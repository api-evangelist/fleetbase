Written by Shiv Thakker · Fleetbase Ledger · v0.7.52 · 8 min read Fleetbase Ledger now supports GNU Taler, the open, privacy-preserving payment system from the GNU project. Generate an invoice, present a taler://pay QR, and the payer settles it from a Taler wallet — no card network, no account, no tracking of the payer. When money needs to go back, refunds return through the wallet the same way.
